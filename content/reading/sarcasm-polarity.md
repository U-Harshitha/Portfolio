---
kind: my-research
title: Enhancing transformers with polarity encoders to detect sarcasm
authors: Harshitha Upadhyayula and co-authors, VNRVJIET
order: 10
---
Sarcasm lives in the gap between what is said and what is felt. Adding a polarity encoder to RoBERTa gave the model explicit sentiment cues, which made that gap easier to see: accuracy went from 85% to 98% on our evaluation.

What it doesn't solve yet: sarcasm that leans on context the text never states, and code-mixed language like Telugu-English.
