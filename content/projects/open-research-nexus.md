GitHub made software visible, forkable and creditable. Research mostly still happens in private drafts until a journal says yes. This borrows the open-source model and renames it for researchers: projects, versions, open questions, proposed revisions, and "build on" for replications that link back to the original.

Projects are public from the first idea, credit is given by CRediT contributor role rather than author order, and open calls let a question find the collaborator it is missing, often from another field.
